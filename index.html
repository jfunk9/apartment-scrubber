Blindspots to consider

  • 20 years in Uptown. You've never lived without a 2-block lake walk. The proximity matters more than the actual use of it. Stone Arch + Mississippi riverfront is your closest Downtown analog — favor North Loop/Mill District for water access.
  • Skyway dependency. Game-changer Nov–March. You've worked downtown and know the system well — value access in cold months. Committing to a non-skyway building is acceptable.
  • Stadium effect. Mill District/Downtown East = 10x/year Vikings home-game energy. You live that vibe of people.
  • North Loop unknown. Called the new Uptown — residence density and some of the most incredible high-end residences. But the vibe is still unknown to you.
  • Music producer noise. Late-night loud speakers = neighbor risk (you on them, them on you). Paper-thin walls are the trap. Concrete vs drywall matters both directions. Dig into construction when down to 3 options.
  • Hidden fees. Luxury towers love amenity fees ($30–75/mo), valet trash ($25), pet rent. Always ask for the "true monthly" number, not just rent.
  • Vintage trap. 2015–2020 luxury buildings have great amenities but thin walls. Pre-1950 conversions (Itasca, Riverwalk, 724 Lofts) are concrete/timber and quieter — at the cost of fewer amenities.
  • Lease length. Class-A towers push 13–15mo leases to hide turn vacancy. Ask up front; 12mo is negotiable on a slow week.
  • Transition cost. 20 years of accumulated stuff. Budget a real moving cost + Goodwill purge. Lakehaus → next place is the rare honest test.
